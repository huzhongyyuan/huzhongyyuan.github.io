I am a Ph.D. student in Control Science and Engineering at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Xiu Li. I received my B.Eng. from Chongqing University.

I work on generative models that connect language, music, human motion, and camera trajectories. Previously, I was a Research Intern at Tencent Games, working on joint human-camera reconstruction.
